## Overview

To authenticate with a ServiceTitan integration environment tenant, you will need:

1. **Client ID** and **Client Secret** – Generated by ServiceTitan when you allow the app to access your integration environment account. Production credentials don't work here.
2. **Tenant ID** – The number that identifies your ServiceTitan account.

This guide walks you through finding each of them.

### Prerequisites

- A ServiceTitan user with the **Manage API Application Access** permission. Ask your ServiceTitan admin to enable it if you don't see **API Application Access** in your settings.
- The app developer must have added your tenant ID to their app. If you don't see the app in Step 1, send them your tenant ID.

### Instructions

#### Step 1: Generate a client ID and secret

1. Log in to your [ServiceTitan integration environment](https://integration.servicetitan.com).
2. Open **Settings**, then go to **Integrations** > **API Application Access**.
3. Click **Connect New App** and choose the app you want to connect.
4. Review the API scopes the app requests, then click **Allow Access**.
5. Under **Application Details**, copy the **Client ID**.
6. Under **Client Secret**, click **Generate** and copy the secret. Treat it like a password: anyone with the client ID and secret can access your ServiceTitan data within the allowed scopes.

#### Step 2: Find your tenant ID

Your tenant ID is a number, for example `1234567890`. The app developer needs it to make the app visible to you in Step 1, so you have usually shared it already. If you don't know it, ask the ServiceTitan admin in your organization.

#### Step 3: Enter credentials in the Connect UI

1. Open the form where you need to authenticate with ServiceTitan.
2. Enter your **Client ID** and **Client Secret** from Step 1.
3. Enter your **Tenant ID** from Step 2.
4. Submit the form to complete authentication.

You are now connected to your ServiceTitan integration environment.

<Note>
  If your organization has several ServiceTitan tenants, repeat these steps for each tenant you want to connect. Each tenant has its own client ID and secret.
</Note>
